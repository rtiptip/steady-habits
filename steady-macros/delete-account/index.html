Steady Macros — Delete your account

Last updated: 2026-11-19.

Steady Macros is published by Steady Habits. This page explains how to delete a Steady Macros account. It is not legal advice.

In the app

Open Steady Macros, go to Settings, then Delete account. The app asks you to type DELETE and confirm. That permanently deletes your account and the data stored with it. It can't be undone.

By email

If you can't use the app, email steadyhabitsapps@gmail.com from the address on the account. Use the subject "Delete my Steady Macros account" and say that you want the account deleted. We'll delete the same server data as an in-app deletion after we confirm the request is from that account.

What is deleted

The server removes the data stored for that account, including the saved plan, the food log, saved meals, custom foods, and the weight log, and it removes the sign-in account itself.

On a phone that completes a confirmed in-app deletion, the app also removes that account's encrypted plan-input history, pending-save file, upgrade reminder, food-log notice, step-sharing on/off marker, cached today's calorie target, weight-trend apply choice and last suggested calories, background-sync switch and the time it last ran, and a downloaded copy of your data. It also cancels the scheduled Health Connect pass. The kilogram or pound choice stays on the phone.

Signing out is not the same as deleting the account. Signing out leaves your saved plan and logs on the server.
